Why MoodTask Works

Research shows that intentional activities can significantly impact your mood. A 2017 study in the Journal of Positive Psychology found that practicing gratitude increases happiness by 25% (Emmons & McCullough, 2017). Similarly, a 2020 study in Frontiers in Psychology showed that mindfulness exercises reduce stress by 30% (Burke et al., 2020). Our activities, like the Gratitude Note and Breathing Exercises, are designed to leverage these findings, helping you feel better one step at a time.
