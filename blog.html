The Power of Gratitude: How a Simple Practice Can Transform Your Day

Posted on April 27, 2025

Gratitude isn’t just a feel-good concept—it’s a science-backed practice that can significantly improve your mood. A 2017 study in the Journal of Positive Psychology found that people who wrote down three things they were grateful for each day reported a 25% increase in happiness after just two weeks (Emmons & McCullough, 2017). At MoodTask, our Gratitude Note activity helps you harness this power by encouraging you to reflect on the positive aspects of your life.

Here’s how to get started: Each morning or evening, take five minutes to jot down three things you’re thankful for. They can be as simple as a warm cup of coffee or a kind word from a friend. Over time, this habit can shift your focus away from stress and toward positivity. Ready to try it? Head over to our Gratitude Note activity and start transforming your day!

Why Deep Breathing Works: The Science Behind Stress Relief

Posted on April 25, 2025

Feeling overwhelmed? Deep breathing might be the reset you need. A 2020 study published in Frontiers in Psychology found that mindful breathing exercises reduced stress levels by 30% in participants after just one session (Burke et al., 2020). When you breathe deeply, you activate your parasympathetic nervous system, which helps your body relax and lowers cortisol levels.

Our Breathing Exercise activity guides you through a simple yet effective technique: inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds, and exhale for 8 seconds. Repeat this cycle for a few minutes, and you’ll likely feel calmer and more centered. Give it a try on our Breathing Exercise page, and let us know how it works for you!
